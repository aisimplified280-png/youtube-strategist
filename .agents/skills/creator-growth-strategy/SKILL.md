# Creator Growth Strategy

Actionable strategies for technical creators in AI and programming.

## Strategic Framework

1. **Underperformance Opportunity Diagnosis**:
   - Compare candidates strictly against format-matched (Shorts vs Long-form) and age-matched videos (first 7, 28, or 90 days).
   - High impressions + low CTR (< median peer CTR) = **Packaging Failure** (Needs title/thumbnail overhaul).
   - Low average view duration / early cliff (<30s) = **Hook/Pacing Failure** (Needs script surgery or chaptering).
   - High CTR + high retention + low views = **Distribution / Saturation Constraint** (Topic narrowness).

2. **Packaging Contrast**:
   - The thumbnail creates curiosity or poses a visual question; the title delivers the promise without repetition.
   - For technical content, replace generic adjectives ("Amazing", "Insane") with concrete technical specifics (model name, latency speedup, token count, benchmark result).

3. **Content Gap & Series Capitalization**:
   - When an upload outperforms the peer baseline by >1.5x, immediately generate 3 follow-up concept angles (e.g., Deep Dive, Beginner Mistakes, Production Deployment).

## Acceptance Criteria

- All recommendations grounded in peer-relative metrics.
- Concrete next steps (e.g. "Test alternative thumbnail focusing on latency metric").
- Maximum 3 prioritized actions per daily briefing to prevent creator overwhelm.
