# AI Research Radar

Systematic discovery of high-value AI topics and developer trends.

## Radar Sources & Tracking

1. **Model Releases & Major Lab Updates**:
   - Track official releases: Google DeepMind, OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta Llama, Mistral.
   - Focus on practical developer impact: context window breakthroughs, pricing changes, speed, agentic capabilities.

2. **Open Source & Tool Ecosystem**:
   - Hugging Face trending models, GitHub trending repositories (LangChain, LlamaIndex, vLLM, Ollama, CrewAI).
   - Evaluate developer friction points: "How do I deploy this locally?", "Is it faster than GPT-4o?", "Production evaluation".

3. **Audience Question Triangulation**:
   - Cross-reference trending AI breakthroughs with recurring questions in channel comments.
   - Example: If viewers frequently ask about local agent setup and a new lightweight quantization drops, flag this as a Priority 1 video concept.

4. **Provenance & Source Linking**:
   - Every radar item must record:
     - Topic / Release title
     - Authoritative source URL
     - Date discovered
     - Why this matters to the AI Simplified Lab creator
     - 2 distinct video angles (e.g., Quick Hands-On vs Architectural Deep Dive)

## Acceptance Criteria

- All radar discoveries cite authoritative original sources with dates.
- Video angles are tailored to the channel's technical profile (Python, AI workflows, real-world utility).
